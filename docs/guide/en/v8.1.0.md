# 8.1.0 — See what a document task changed, and more examples to try

**A snapshot as of 8.1.0 (released 2026-10-01).** It will go stale as the app moves on — that is
expected, and the living reference is the guide each section links to.

Nothing to configure. Everything here is in **Blueprints**' document tasks.

## New features

### See each document against its original

- When a document task finishes, its screen shows, under the report, each document the build kept an original
  of (`.blueprint/originals/`) compared with the file now ([#2793](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2793)),
  so what changed can be read in place, with the sentences around it.
- A review answered 「直し方の案まで作る」 writes a proposed copy beside each document (`<name>.proposed<ext>`);
  those are compared with their documents too ([#2795](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2795)).
- The comparison follows the app's theme, light and custom themes included
  ([#2797](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2797)).

### Carry on with 「文書を整える」 (polish)

- Polish can work through the findings adopt shelved ([#2781](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2781)):
  answer 「棚上げした指摘も直す」 to 「棚上げした指摘も直しますか」.
- The finished screen of 「文書のフォルダに chaff を入れる」 (adopt) offers polish on the same places
  ([#2783](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2783)).
- The finished screen of 「用語をそろえる」 (glossary) offers polish on the same documents
  ([#2765](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2765)).

### Tasks look further

- Polish reads specifications and READMEs for what their readers need
  ([#2779](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2779)).
- 「文書を確かめる」 (verify) checks unit price × quantity and tax, not only totals
  ([#2763](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2763)).

### Examples to try without documents of your own

The new-build form's examples gain:

- 「規程を規程として整える」 — a regulation ([#2755](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2755))
- 「英語のブログ記事を整える」 — an English blog post; the document stays in English, the report comes back in your
  language ([#2773](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2773))
- 「出張旅費規程の改定を比べる」 — a regulation revision ([#2757](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2757))
- 「検討報告を部長向けに要約する」 — a report summarised for a named reader ([#2759](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2759))
- 「見積書を確かめる」 — an estimate ([#2761](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2761))
- 「ネットショップの利用規約を読み解く」 — terms of service ([#2767](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2767))
- 「休暇の規程と手引きに尋ねる」 — asking two documents that disagree ([#2769](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2769))

More in the [Blueprints guide](blueprints.html).

## What looks different

- The new-build form shows one example per task, and the rest open with 「ほかの例を見る」
  ([#2785](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2785)) — the examples had pushed the task choice far down.
- Document tasks are listed most-used first ([#2787](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2787)).
- Polish's report puts the questions for the writer right after what was polished
  ([#2789](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2789)).
- 「規約をつくる」 (style) starts its language and strictness questions on the usual answers
  ([#2771](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2771)).

## Under the hood

- When glossary creates chaff.yaml for documents of different kinds, it gives each its own genre
  ([#2775](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2775)), so a policy's guide is no longer measured as a policy.
- Polish no longer reports a missing STYLE.md when only chaff.yaml is needed
  ([#2777](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2777)).
- The document tasks run chaffjs 0.17 ([#2791](https://github.com/receptron/mulmoterminal/pull/2791)).

Nothing here needs doing.
