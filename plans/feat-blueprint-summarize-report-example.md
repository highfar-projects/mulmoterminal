# A summarize example: a report, part by part, for a named reader (#2758)

summarize's only example used 「短く（5 文まで）」 and left the reader and the focus empty. This adds a self-written
review report (replacing the company chat tool) summarized 「くわしく（部分ごとに）」 for 「会議に出ていない部長」, with
「費用と、いつから使えるか」 as the focus, so all three of those answers are shown working.

The report has five sections under one title, prices per person and a yearly total for 280 people that agree
(280 × 600 × 12 = 2,016,000; 280 × 450 × 12 = 1,512,000; the difference 504,000), and dated steps.

A real build from the example finished: the summary opened with the focus (when and how much), covered every section,
and backed each sentence with a quotation whose numbers the check matched.
