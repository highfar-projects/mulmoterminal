# A polish example for an English blog post (#2772)

Every polish example was Japanese. This adds a self-written English tech blog post (`presets/english-blog/post.md`)
polished as 「ブログ（技術記事）」, to show that the genre's measures (`--genre blog/tech`) and the blog viewpoints work
on English, and that the document stays in its language while the report comes back in the person's.

Under blog/tech chaff reports three things in English: a padded opening, a sentence too long, and a cliched closing.
The conclusion only restates the post and names nothing concrete, for the viewpoints to turn into questions.

A real build from the example (screen in Japanese) removed the opening and the cliche, split the long sentence without
changing a fact, and reported the empty conclusion and the thin section as questions for the writer, in Japanese.
